Why self-host your SMS?

A message is more than a transaction — it's a one-time code, a reminder, an alert. Here's how this gateway is built around keeping that in your hands.

📱

Your own number

Messages send through a real SIM card in a phone you control — not a shared pool shared with other customers.

🔒

End-to-end encryption

Message content and recipient numbers can be encrypted on-device before they ever reach the server.

⚡

Live delivery

A persistent connection to the device means messages go out immediately — no polling, no delay.

📡

Webhooks, not polling

Get a signed HTTP callback the moment a message is sent, delivered, or fails — build on events, not loops.

📄

MMS included

Images, audio, video and other attachments send the same way text does, through the same API.

🤝

You control the data

Self-hosted means your message history, device list and credentials stay on infrastructure you run.
